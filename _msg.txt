fix(auth,profile): success mark, OTP paste, graduation year, free-text university

- Email verified: the green disc was a 480px-wide ellipse because the auth
  polish rule forces width:100% on every direct child of the form column.
  The mark is now the shared SuccessCheck (blue disc, animated check and
  ring) from the design, and that rule skips the round marks.
- Verification code: pasting a code filled only the first box. A paste (or
  an autofilled value) now spreads its digits across all six, ignoring
  spaces and dashes, with one-time-code autocomplete on the first box.
- Profile setup: the expected-graduation field is gone for a graduate (they
  have nothing left to expect, and the field was blocking submission), and
  the year list starts at the current year - a past year is not an
  expectation.
- Profile setup: the university list is not exhaustive, so the combobox now
  accepts a typed name that is not in it (SearchableSelect allowCustom).
